Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Vertex Capital Projects Pte. Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your personal data and complying with the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect through vertex-sg.com, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights.

Personal data we collect

We collect information you provide directly — for example your name, company, email address, telephone number and the contents of your message when you use our contact form or email us. We also collect limited technical data automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited and the date and time of access, through cookies and analytics.

Purposes

We use personal data to respond to your enquiries, provide and improve our services, operate and secure our website, send information you have requested, and comply with legal obligations.

Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with service providers that help us operate our website and communications, bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations, and with authorities where required by law.

Transfer, storage and retention

Your data may be processed in Singapore or other jurisdictions with appropriate safeguards. Enquiry records are retained for up to 24 months after last contact unless a relationship continues. Analytics data is retained according to provider settings, typically 14 months.

Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures, including access controls, encryption in transit and secure hosting.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, and may withdraw consent. Contact projects@vertex-sg.com.
