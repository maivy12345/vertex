COMMON QUESTIONS

How the work
fits your programme.

The starting point depends on the decisions you need to make and the quality of information already available.

Do we need a full PMO to work with Vertex?

No. A focused review of a schedule, forecast or governance issue can be a useful first step. The scope should match the decision at hand.

Can existing systems and reports be used?

Yes. We start by understanding current sources, definitions and reporting routines, then identify what needs to be connected or improved.

How is project intelligence different from a dashboard?

A dashboard shows movement. Decision-ready intelligence adds cause, implication, options, ownership and timing so a team can act.

How does the work stay useful after mobilisation?

Clear ownership, review cycles and capability transfer help the programme maintain its controls as conditions change.
