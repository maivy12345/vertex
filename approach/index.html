Our approach

A disciplined, repeatable controls cycle.

Data flows in from the project team, is validated, analysed and turned into reporting and recommendations — on a fixed cadence so nothing is missed.

Five-step controls cycle

01

Intake & validation

We agree the data interfaces up front and validate progress, cost and resource data before anything is reported — garbage in is never allowed to become garbage out.

02

Schedule & cost analysis

The P6 schedule is updated, the critical path is reassessed and Earned Value indices are computed. Baselines change only through a controlled, logged process.

03

Risk & forecast

Risks are reviewed and rescored; EAC and ETC forecasts and confidence ranges are refreshed. Forecasts are presented as ranges, not false-precision numbers.

04

Reporting

Dashboards refresh and the executive report is issued with a clear narrative and recommended actions. The report leads with decisions, not a wall of data.

05

Review & decision

We present findings to the project board, agree actions and track them to closure in the next cycle.

Independence

Separate from the work we measure.

Our role is project and programme controls. We are deliberately separate from the parties whose work we measure, review or model, because that separation makes our output credible to owners, lenders and auditors.

All deliverables and project data we produce belong to the client. We protect confidential information and apply appropriate security throughout the engagement.
