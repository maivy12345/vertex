About Vertex

Independent controls. Decision-ready intelligence.

A Singapore-based project and programme controls consultancy supporting owners, developers and main contractors across the region.

Who we are

Senior specialists, close to the work.

Vertex Capital Projects supports large infrastructure and industrial programmes with the scheduling, cost-control, risk and reporting backbone that keeps complex projects on track.

Our work is performed by senior planners, cost engineers and risk analysts using industry-standard tools — Primavera P6 for scheduling, Earned Value Management for cost performance, and Power BI or Tableau for executive reporting. We integrate data validated by the project team and turn it into clear, decision-ready intelligence.

We are deliberately a controls and advisory firm, not a construction contractor. That separation of duties allows our reporting to stay independent and credible — to the client, to lenders and to auditors.

Our direction

Evidence first. Always.

We commit to telling the truth about a project early, clearly and with evidence — even when the truth is uncomfortable.

Mission

A single, trusted view

To give every major programme a single, trusted view of schedule, cost and risk — so decisions are made on evidence.

Vision

A partner for critical delivery

To be the controls partner owners and lenders rely on when a project absolutely has to land on time and on budget.

Commitment

Early, clear and honest

Good news delivered late helps no one. An honest, timely picture is the most valuable thing a specialist can offer.
