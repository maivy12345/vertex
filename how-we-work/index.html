How we work

Clear terms. No surprises.

How an engagement runs, how fees work, and who owns the data, deliverables and methods involved. The written proposal for each engagement sets out the binding terms.

Engagement

From first conversation to handover.

Every engagement is different, but the steps are consistent.

01

Initial conversation

We discuss the project stage, the decision at hand and the information available. There is no charge and no obligation.

02

Written proposal

Scope, deliverables, team, cadence, timing, fees and acceptance steps are set out in writing before work starts.

03

Delivery and handover

We work the agreed controls cycle, report on a fixed cadence and hand over the agreed deliverables at the end.

Fees

Fees are quoted for each engagement after scoping and confirmed in the proposal.

  • Fixed fee for defined-scope work such as health checks, independent reviews and PMO set-up.
  • Periodic rate for ongoing controls support, based on the agreed team and cadence.
  • Expenses and third-party licences are charged only where agreed in the proposal.
  • Payment milestones and terms are stated in the proposal.

Deliverables and transfer

Deliverables are the specific outputs listed in the proposal — for example a baseline schedule, a cost forecast, a risk analysis report or a dashboard.

  • Ownership of project-specific deliverables transfers to the client once the related fees have been paid in full.
  • Until then, the client may use the deliverables for the purposes of the engagement.
  • At handover we provide the agreed deliverables and working files in the formats set out in the proposal.

Methods and tools we retain

Vertex retains ownership of its pre-existing methods, templates, models, software tools and know-how, including improvements made to them during an engagement.

  • Where these are built into a deliverable, the client receives a licence to use them for its project.
  • This lets us bring proven tools to every project without charging to rebuild them.

Client data and confidentiality

  • Data the client provides remains the client’s at all times and is used only to deliver the engagement.
  • Access is limited to the people working on the engagement, with appropriate security throughout.
  • At the end of the engagement we return or delete client data as instructed, subject to records we must keep by law.

Independence

We do not design, build or supply the work we measure, review or model. We disclose any potential conflict of interest before accepting an engagement, so our reporting stays credible to owners, lenders and auditors.

Questions

Answers to common questions about services, fees and ownership are in our FAQ. For anything specific to your project, get in touch.
