Master Schedule & Programme Control

We build, baseline and maintain the integrated programme that everything else measures against.

The challenge

Without a single, logic-driven schedule that everyone measures against, “on track” becomes an opinion rather than a fact.

Our approach

We build the master schedule in Primavera P6 from a properly structured Work Breakdown Structure, establish a frozen baseline, and maintain it on a fixed update cycle.

What we do

  • Logic-driven WBS and baseline schedule in Primavera P6
  • Critical-path and near-critical-path analysis
  • Progress measurement and variance against baseline
  • Recovery and acceleration scenarios
  • Interface and milestone management across work packages

Key deliverables

What you receive, as distinct from the work we carry out:

  • Integrated master schedule in Primavera P6, with WBS and activity coding
  • Approved, frozen baseline with a basis-of-schedule document
  • Periodic schedule update with critical-path and variance narrative
  • Milestone and interface register shared across work packages
  • Recovery or acceleration scenario reports when required

Standards we apply

  • PMI Practice Standard for Scheduling
  • AACE recommended practices for schedule levels of detail and critical-path identification
  • DCMA 14-point assessment as a schedule-quality check
  • Controlled baseline change process, logged and approved

Typical engagement

Scope, team and duration are agreed in writing for each project. A typical arrangement looks like this:

Starting point
At mobilisation, or on a programme already underway
Cadence
Regular working updates and a periodic formal report
Format
Embedded planner, remote support or a combination

Who it’s for

  • Owners and developers who need one integrated programme across packages
  • Main contractors who need a defensible contract programme
  • Lenders’ and owners’ advisors who need a verifiable baseline

Outcome

A defensible, transparent programme that the whole team—and external reviewers—can trust.
