Cost Control & Earned Value Management

We translate work done into financial performance and a credible forecast to completion.

The challenge

Knowing you have spent money is easy; knowing whether you are getting value for it is hard.

Our approach

We cost-load the baseline, compute CPI and SPI from validated progress, and forecast Estimate at Completion conservatively so the indices warn early rather than flatter.

What we do

  • Cost baseline, cash-flow S-curve and resource loading
  • CPI and SPI performance indices
  • EAC and ETC forecasting
  • Variance analysis with corrective actions
  • Change and trend tracking against budget

Key deliverables

What you receive, as distinct from the work we carry out:

  • Cost-loaded, time-phased performance baseline
  • Cash-flow S-curve and funding forecast
  • Periodic Earned Value report with CPI, SPI and variance commentary
  • EAC and ETC forecast with its basis and a forecast range
  • Change and trend log reconciled to the approved budget

Standards we apply

  • Earned Value Management principles aligned with ANSI/EIA-748
  • PMI Standard for Earned Value Management
  • AACE Total Cost Management framework and recommended practices
  • Progress claimed only for work complete and accepted

Typical engagement

Scope, team and duration are agreed in writing for each project. A typical arrangement looks like this:

Starting point
Once a baseline schedule and budget exist, or alongside them
Cadence
Aligned to the project’s cost and valuation cycle
Format

Who it’s for

  • Owners and lenders who need a credible forecast to completion
  • Contractors protecting margin on large packages
  • Finance teams reconciling payments with physical progress

Outcome

Leadership sees, early and clearly, whether the project will finish on budget—and what to do if not.
