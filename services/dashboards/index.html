Services / Service 04

Executive Dashboards & Reporting

We turn project data into a live, visual story leaders can act on in minutes.

The challenge

Leaders often receive long reports assembled by hand from different spreadsheets. By the time they are read, the data is out of date and the decision being asked for is unclear.

Our approach

We build Power BI or Tableau dashboards from the controlled data set and consolidate each period into a concise executive report with recommended actions.

What we do

  • Live Power BI or Tableau dashboards
  • Monthly executive reports covering schedule, cost, risk and KPIs
  • Programme health indices and trend visualisation
  • Tailored views for owners, lenders and boards
  • Reporting data governance and maintenance

Key deliverables

What you receive, as distinct from the work we carry out:

  • Live dashboard in Power BI or Tableau, connected to the controlled data set
  • Periodic executive report with narrative and recommended actions
  • KPI definitions and data dictionary
  • Audience-specific views for owners, lenders and boards
  • Reporting calendar and data-governance procedure

Standards we apply

  • One validated, controlled data set behind every view
  • Documented KPI definitions and data lineage
  • Earned Value and schedule metrics calculated consistently with recognised practice
  • Role-based access to client data

Typical engagement

Scope, team and duration are agreed in writing for each project. A typical arrangement looks like this:

Starting point
Once schedule and cost data are under control
Cadence
Dashboard refresh each cycle; formal report each period
Format
Built and maintained by Vertex, or handed over to your team

Who it’s for

  • Boards and executives who need a current, concise picture
  • Lenders and investors with formal reporting obligations
  • Portfolio owners comparing several projects side by side

Outcome

Stakeholders get a clear, current picture without chasing spreadsheets.
