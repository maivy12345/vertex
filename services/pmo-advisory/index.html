PMO Advisory & Independent Reviews

We help set up the controls function and provide an objective second opinion when it matters.

The challenge

When a programme is under pressure, or before a major decision, leaders need to know whether the plan and the numbers can be trusted—from someone with no stake in the answer.

Our approach

We establish controls processes, templates and governance, and provide schedule and cost health checks, delay analysis and stage-gate reviews independent of delivery.

What we do

  • PMO and project-controls set-up
  • Independent schedule and cost assurance
  • Delay analysis and forensic schedule support
  • Stage-gate and readiness reviews
  • Capability building and handover

Key deliverables

What you receive, as distinct from the work we carry out:

  • Project-controls procedures, templates and governance structure
  • Schedule and cost health-check report with rated findings and recommendations
  • Delay analysis report with a stated methodology
  • Stage-gate or readiness review report
  • Training material and a handover pack for your team

Standards we apply

  • AACE recommended practice for forensic schedule analysis
  • Society of Construction Law Delay and Disruption Protocol
  • DCMA 14-point schedule assessment
  • ISO 21502 and PMI guidance on project and programme management

Typical engagement

Scope, team and duration are agreed in writing for each project. A typical arrangement looks like this:

Starting point
At set-up, before a decision gate, or when performance is in question
Cadence
Fixed-scope review or phased advisory support
Format
Independent review team reporting to the sponsor

Who it’s for

  • Owners establishing or strengthening a PMO
  • Lenders and investors who need independent assurance
  • Organisations facing recovery, claims or disputes

Outcome

A controls function that is fit for purpose, and assurance that the numbers are real.
