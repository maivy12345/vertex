Services / Service 03

Risk Management & Analytics

We make uncertainty visible and quantifiable so it can be managed, not feared.

The challenge

Many risk registers are written once and reviewed rarely. They list concerns, but say little about how likely the finish date or budget really is—or where contingency should go.

Our approach

Each risk is scored by probability and impact, assigned an owner and tracked to closure. Monte Carlo analysis turns schedule and cost uncertainty into confidence ranges.

What we do

  • Strategic risk register, scoring and ownership
  • Monte Carlo schedule and cost simulation
  • Mitigation planning and closure tracking
  • Risk-adjusted contingency and float analysis
  • Board-level risk reporting

Key deliverables

What you receive, as distinct from the work we carry out:

  • Risk management plan with an agreed scoring matrix
  • Live risk register with owners, actions and due dates
  • Quantitative schedule and cost risk analysis report with confidence levels
  • Contingency recommendation tied to quantified risk
  • Periodic board-level risk summary

Standards we apply

  • ISO 31000 risk management framework
  • AACE recommended practices for integrated cost and schedule risk analysis and contingency
  • PMI Standard for Risk Management in Portfolios, Programs and Projects
  • Ranges and confidence levels reported instead of single-point promises

Typical engagement

Scope, team and duration are agreed in writing for each project. A typical arrangement looks like this:

Starting point
Before a funding or approval gate, or as an ongoing function
Cadence
Regular register reviews and analysis at key milestones
Format
Facilitated workshops plus independent analysis

Who it’s for

  • Owners setting contingency and seeking approvals
  • Programmes facing funding gates or lender due diligence
  • Projects with complex interfaces and long-lead items

Outcome

Decisions are made with a clear-eyed view of what could go wrong and how likely it is.
