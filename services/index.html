Services

Project controls that keep complex programmes on track.

Five connected services, delivered to recognised standards by senior specialists.

01

Master Schedule & Programme Control

We build, baseline and maintain the integrated programme that everything else measures against.

The challenge

Without a single, logic-driven schedule that everyone measures against, “on track” becomes an opinion rather than a fact.

Our approach

We build the master schedule in Primavera P6 from a properly structured Work Breakdown Structure, establish a frozen baseline, and maintain it on a fixed update cycle.

What we do

  • Logic-driven WBS and baseline schedule in Primavera P6
  • Critical-path and near-critical-path analysis
  • Progress measurement and variance against baseline
  • Recovery and acceleration scenarios
  • Interface and milestone management across work packages

Outcome

A defensible, transparent programme that the whole team — and external reviewers — can trust.

02

Cost Control & Earned Value Management

We translate work done into financial performance and a credible forecast to completion.

The challenge

Knowing you have spent money is easy; knowing whether you are getting value for it is hard.

Our approach

We cost-load the baseline, compute CPI and SPI from validated progress, and forecast Estimate at Completion conservatively so the indices warn early rather than flatter.

What we do

  • Cost baseline, cash-flow S-curve and resource loading
  • CPI and SPI performance indices
  • EAC and ETC forecasting
  • Variance analysis with corrective actions
  • Change and trend tracking against budget

Outcome

Leadership sees, early and clearly, whether the project will finish on budget — and what to do if not.

03

Risk Management & Analytics

We make uncertainty visible and quantifiable so it can be managed, not feared.

Our approach

Each risk is scored by probability and impact, assigned an owner and tracked to closure. Monte Carlo analysis turns schedule and cost uncertainty into confidence ranges.

What we do

  • Strategic risk register, scoring and ownership
  • Monte Carlo schedule and cost simulation
  • Mitigation planning and closure tracking
  • Risk-adjusted contingency and float analysis
  • Board-level risk reporting

Outcome

Decisions are made with a clear-eyed view of what could go wrong and how likely it is.

04

Executive Dashboards & Reporting

We turn project data into a live, visual story leaders can act on in minutes.

Our approach

We build Power BI or Tableau dashboards from the controlled data set and consolidate each period into a concise executive report with recommended actions.

What we do

  • Live Power BI or Tableau dashboards
  • Monthly executive reports covering schedule, cost, risk and KPIs
  • Programme health indices and trend visualisation
  • Tailored views for owners, lenders and boards
  • Reporting data governance and maintenance

Outcome

Stakeholders get a clear, current picture without chasing spreadsheets.

05

PMO Advisory & Independent Reviews

We help set up the controls function and provide an objective second opinion when it matters.

Our approach

We establish controls processes, templates and governance, and provide schedule and cost health checks, delay analysis and stage-gate reviews independent of delivery.

What we do

  • PMO and project-controls set-up
  • Independent schedule and cost assurance
  • Delay analysis and forensic schedule support
  • Stage-gate and readiness reviews
  • Capability building and handover

Outcome

A controls function that is fit for purpose, and assurance that the numbers are real.
