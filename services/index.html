Service 01

Master Schedule &
Programme Control

We build, baseline and maintain the integrated programme that everything else measures against.

The challenge

Without a single, logic-driven schedule that everyone measures against, “on track” becomes an opinion rather than a fact.

Our approach

We build the master schedule in Primavera P6 from a properly structured Work Breakdown Structure, establish a frozen baseline, and maintain it on a fixed update cycle.

What we do
  • 01Logic-driven WBS and baseline schedule in Primavera P6
  • 02Critical-path and near-critical-path analysis
  • 03Progress measurement and variance against baseline
  • 04Recovery and acceleration scenarios
  • 05Interface and milestone management across work packages
Outcome

A defensible, transparent programme that the whole team—and external reviewers—can trust.

Service 02

Cost Control &
Earned Value

We translate work done into financial performance and a credible forecast to completion.

The challenge

Knowing you have spent money is easy; knowing whether you are getting value for it is hard.

Our approach

We cost-load the baseline, compute CPI and SPI from validated progress, and forecast Estimate at Completion conservatively so the indices warn early rather than flatter.

What we do
  • 01Cost baseline, cash-flow S-curve and resource loading
  • 02CPI and SPI performance indices
  • 03EAC and ETC forecasting
  • 04Variance analysis with corrective actions
  • 05Change and trend tracking against budget
Outcome

Leadership sees, early and clearly, whether the project will finish on budget—and what to do if not.

Service 03

Risk Management
& Analytics

We make uncertainty visible and quantifiable so it can be managed, not feared.

Our approach

Each risk is scored by probability and impact, assigned an owner and tracked to closure. Monte Carlo analysis turns schedule and cost uncertainty into confidence ranges.

What we do
  • 01Strategic risk register, scoring and ownership
  • 02Monte Carlo schedule and cost simulation
  • 03Mitigation planning and closure tracking
  • 04Risk-adjusted contingency and float analysis
  • 05Board-level risk reporting
Outcome

Decisions are made with a clear-eyed view of what could go wrong and how likely it is.

Executive Dashboards
& Reporting

Our approach

What we do
  • 01Live Power BI or Tableau dashboards
  • 02Monthly executive reports covering schedule, cost, risk and KPIs
  • 03Programme health indices and trend visualisation
  • 04Tailored views for owners, lenders and boards
  • 05Reporting data governance and maintenance
Outcome

Stakeholders get a clear, current picture without chasing spreadsheets.
