FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers about our services, how engagements work, fees, and who owns what. For the full working terms, see How we work.

About Vertex and our services

What does Vertex do?

We are a Singapore-based project and programme controls consultancy. We provide five services: Master Schedule & Programme Control, Cost Control & Earned Value Management, Risk Management & Analytics, Executive Dashboards & Reporting and PMO Advisory & Independent Reviews.

Do you design or build projects as well?

No. Our role is controls, reporting and advisory only. We are deliberately separate from the parties whose work we measure, which keeps our reporting objective.

Which sectors and locations do you work in?

We support industrial parks and infrastructure, energy and utilities, commercial and mixed-use developments, and public and institutional projects, from our base in Singapore and across the region.

Can you join a project that is already underway?

Yes. We can start at mobilisation or stand up controls on a project already moving, beginning with a review of the current schedule, cost and risk information.

Which tools and standards do you use?

Primavera P6 for scheduling, Earned Value Management for cost performance, and Power BI or Tableau for reporting. Our work follows PMI and AACE recommended practices and the ISO 31000 risk management framework. Each service page lists the standards that apply to it.

Engagements and fees

How does an engagement start?

With a conversation about the project stage, the decision at hand and the information available. We then issue a written proposal setting out scope, deliverables, team, timing and fees. Work starts once the proposal is agreed.

How are your fees structured?

Fees are quoted for each engagement after scoping. Defined-scope work, such as a health check or PMO set-up, is usually quoted as a fixed fee. Ongoing controls support is usually charged at an agreed periodic rate. Expenses and any third-party software licences are charged only where agreed in the proposal.

Is there a charge for an initial conversation?

No. The initial conversation and the written proposal are free of charge and carry no obligation.

Can we engage just one service?

Yes. Each service works on its own, or together with the others as a complete controls function.

Do you work on site or remotely?

Either, or a combination. The working arrangement is agreed in the proposal to suit the project.

How often will we receive reports?

We work on a fixed cadence agreed at the start: a regular working rhythm plus a periodic formal report. The frequency is set to match the project’s decision cycle.

Deliverables, data and ownership

Who owns the deliverables?

Ownership of the project-specific deliverables we produce for you transfers to you once the related fees have been paid in full. Until then you may use them for the purposes of the engagement.

What about your methods, templates and tools?

Vertex retains ownership of its pre-existing methods, templates, models, software tools and know-how. Where these are built into a deliverable, you receive a licence to use them for your project.

Who owns our project data?

You do, at all times. Data you provide to us remains yours, and we use it only to deliver the engagement.

How do you protect confidential information?

We keep client information confidential, restrict access to the people working on the engagement and apply appropriate security throughout. Confidentiality terms can be set out in the proposal or a separate agreement.

What happens at the end of an engagement?

We hand over the agreed deliverables and working files, then return or delete your data as you instruct, subject to any records we must keep by law.

Independence and assurance

How do you stay independent?

We do not design, build or supply the work we measure, and we disclose any potential conflict of interest before we accept an engagement.

Can you report to lenders or investors?

Yes. We can structure reporting for lenders, investors and boards, and provide independent assurance on schedule and cost.

Can you help with delay claims or disputes?

We provide delay analysis and forensic schedule support. We do not provide legal advice; we work alongside your legal advisers where needed.
